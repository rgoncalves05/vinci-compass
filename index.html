Team equity (VSOP)

VSOP = Virtual Stock-Option Program. Why it matters in New York:

The problem investors will see

The company’s value sits in the technical team that built the product and the Match engine. If these people only have a salary, the investor thinks: “if they leave, who runs the product?” That is a risk, so the investor either stays out or comes in paying less.

The solution: a VSOP (virtual equity plan)

  • Pool: about 10% of the company is set aside for the team.
  • No new shareholders: nobody becomes a partner. When the company is sold, each person receives their share in cash.
  • Vesting: the share is earned over 4 years. Whoever leaves early takes nothing.
  • Control: Luciana stays in control of the company.

Analogy: it’s like giving the groom a percentage of the mare’s sale. He doesn’t own her, but he stays and takes good care of her until she is sold.

The line for New York

“We’re setting up a 10% VSOP for the team, with 4-year vesting.”

Em português

VSOP = Virtual Stock-Option Program. Por que importa em Nova York:

O problema que o investidor vai ver

O valor da empresa está no time técnico que construiu o produto e o motor do Match. Se essas pessoas só têm salário, o investidor pensa: “se elas saírem, quem toca o produto?” Isso é risco, e o investidor ou não entra ou entra pagando menos.

A solução, o VSOP (plano de participação virtual)

  • Reserva: separa-se cerca de 10% da empresa para o time.
  • Sem sócios novos: ninguém vira sócio. Na venda da empresa, cada um recebe em dinheiro a sua parte.
  • Vesting: a parte é conquistada ao longo de 4 anos. Quem sai cedo não leva nada.
  • Controle: a Luciana continua no controle da empresa.

Analogia: é como dar ao tratador uma porcentagem da venda da égua. Ele não é dono, mas fica e cuida bem dela até a venda.

A frase para Nova York

“We’re setting up a 10% VSOP for the team, with 4-year vesting.”

Three rules

  1. Listen first: ask what they invest in before you tell your story.
  2. Keep every answer under 30 seconds, then ask a question back.
  3. If you don’t know a number, say “I’ll send you the exact figures.”

After each conversation

Write down the name, what they said, and the next step. Within 24 hours send a two-line note, the one-pager and the website. Send the full deck when they ask for it.
