Vinci Compass Alternative Investments Week · Nov 9–12 · Luciana

Your opener: pick one

Who to talk to

Be friendly, don’t pitch

Sebastien Sherman, Dan Oneglia, Riad Abrahams, Chris Taylor, Mitchell Goldstein, Henry Deng, Daniel Loeb.

A greeting and one question about their work is enough.

Three rules

  1. Listen first: ask what they invest in before you tell your story.
  2. Keep every answer under 30 seconds, then ask a question back.
  3. If you don’t know a number, say “I’ll send you the exact figures.”

If asked about money

“We’re raising 1.6 million dollars. I’ll send the details and the deck.”

Don’t say what the company is worth unless the numbers were agreed beforehand.

After each conversation

Write down the name, what they said, and the next step. Send a two-line note, the deck and the website within 24 hours.
